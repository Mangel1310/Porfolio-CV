## Platform

web

## Stack
React + Vite

## Users
Technical recruiters, hiring managers, and engineering team leads evaluating professional engineering capability, problem-solving, and culture fit for software engineering roles.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio and interactive CV that effectively communicates engineering competence, real-world project impact, technical depth, and work history, converting visits into interviews and job offers.

## Positioning
An authentic, production-grade engineer's portfolio—focused on verifiable project outcomes, clear architectural thinking, clean code craft, and an immediate feel for engineering excellence rather than generic buzzwords.

## Operating Context
Quick initial scan (30-60 seconds) by recruiters screening dozens of candidates, followed by deeper technical inspection by engineering leads looking at live demos, architecture decisions, and code quality. Primarily viewed on desktop/laptop during evaluation, with responsive mobile access during quick shares or LinkedIn clicks.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Fast load times, responsive across all screen sizes.
- Interactive showcase of featured projects with live links, source repositories, and technical breakdowns.
- Clear, structured CV / experience timeline highlighting achievements and tech stack.
- Direct contact pathways (email, LinkedIn, GitHub).
- Stack: React + Vite (SPA).
